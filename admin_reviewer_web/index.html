FacultyLink

CONNECT · EVALUATE · ADVANCE.

FacultyLink is a faculty self-assessment platform designed to support document organization, KRA-based scoring, reviewer verification, and rank upgrade simulation. It helps streamline preparation while maintaining human review and alignment with applicable evaluation guidelines.

Human review

Reviewer feedback, correction requests, and verified points remain central to every indicator.

Guideline aligned

Structured around DBM–CHED Joint Circular No. 3, s. 2022 (Enhanced Implementing Guidelines).

Supports, not replaces

Official rank decisions stay with authorized committees and DBM–CHED processes.
